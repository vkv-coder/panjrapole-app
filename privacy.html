Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What We Collect
  • Accountant/admin account details — name, email (used to sign in and identify who entered each receipt)
  • Donor details entered on each receipt — name, WhatsApp number, city, and optionally address, email, PAN (PAN is only collected when the donor requests an 80G tax receipt)
  • Donation details — amount, donation head(s), payment mode, and any transaction/cheque reference you provide

We do not collect your location, contacts, or any device data beyond what's listed above.

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored on Supabase's hosted infrastructure, protected by row-level security rules so each accountant only sees the receipts they personally entered, and only admins can see every receipt.

Who Can Access Your Data

Donor and donation details are visible only to the Panjrapole's own admin and accountant accounts — never to the public, and never shared with any third party. Account passwords are stored securely (hashed) — we cannot read them.

Data Deletion

To request correction or deletion of any donor/donation record, or an accountant account, contact the Panjrapole admin, or write to info.anyapps@gmail.com — requests are handled within 7 working days.
